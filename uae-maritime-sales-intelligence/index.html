Why UAE

A dense maritime market creates opportunity — and a lot of noise.

The UAE hosts vessel operators, ship managers, offshore businesses, shipyards, marine service companies, distributors and procurement teams. That makes static prospecting easy to start but difficult to prioritize. Merqiva focuses on qualification: which account fits the offer, what changed, who matters and what the next action should be.
