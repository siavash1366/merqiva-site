Why Now

A market or fleet change can create a commercial hypothesis, but it does not automatically create a purchase. Merqiva’s job is to explain why the signal may matter for the supplier’s exact product and what evidence supports that conclusion.

Recommended Action

A real brief should translate the research into an appropriate next step: verify a buyer role, prepare a product-specific sales angle, monitor the signal, or prioritize outreach when the evidence justifies it.

Evidence Standard

Material claims should be separated into VERIFIED FACT, INFERENCE or UNKNOWN, with source links and last-verified context where applicable. Merqiva does not guarantee replies, meetings, contracts or sales outcomes.
