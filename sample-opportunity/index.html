Why Now

A new or expanded fleet can create procurement windows for equipment, systems, services and supporting vendors. Merqiva explains the timing signal and the evidence behind it so a sales team can decide whether outreach is warranted.

Recommended Action

Review the evidence, verify the decision maker, prepare a product-specific sales angle, and contact the account within the recommended timing window.

Evidence Standard

Each material claim should be separated into VERIFIED FACT, INFERENCE, or UNKNOWN, with source links and a last-verified date where applicable.
