Why offshore needs qualification

A large offshore operator can still be a poor prospect if the fleet, timing or buyer context does not fit what you sell.

Merqiva’s opportunity model is designed to combine account fit with observable activity. The goal is to explain why a supplier should spend time on one operator now rather than treat every company as equally valuable.

Fleet and vessel fit

Prioritize operators and managers according to relevant vessel classes and operating context.

Commercial change

Supplier-specific reasoning

Explain why the account matters for the customer’s exact equipment or service rather than offering a generic offshore-company list.
