DATA & EVIDENCE

Evidence is part of the product.

Merqiva is designed so buyers can understand not only what an opportunity says, but why it was considered relevant — and what remains uncertain.

01

Source traceability

Material claims should link back to identified public sources where appropriate, with verification context recorded when practical.

02

Fact vs inference

We distinguish what a source states from what the analysis concludes. Unknowns remain unknown instead of being filled in.

03

Freshness & confidence

Decision-maker and signal information is treated according to the strength, relevance and recency of available evidence.

VERIFIED FACT

A material claim supported by an identified source where available.

INFERENCE

An analytical conclusion that is explicitly labeled and should be reviewed by the customer.

UNKNOWN

Information that has not been verified and is not presented as fact.

What a real opportunity brief should expose

Signal detectedWhen the relevant change or event was observed.
Last verifiedWhen important account or buyer information was last checked.
Sources checkedLinks or source references supporting material claims.
ConfidenceHow strongly the evidence supports the qualification.

What Merqiva does not claim

Public-source research can change. Contact roles can move. Signals can be ambiguous. Scores can help prioritize but cannot guarantee a reply, meeting, contract, purchase or revenue. Customers remain responsible for reviewing research before outreach.
