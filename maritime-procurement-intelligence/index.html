Procurement is not one contact

Complex maritime purchases often involve multiple roles, timing signals and technical context.

For many marine products and services, a useful sales opportunity requires more than a procurement email address. The commercial path can involve purchasing, technical, fleet, operations, project or management stakeholders. Merqiva researches the role most relevant to the opportunity and separates what is verified from what is inferred.

01

Buyer-role research

Identify relevant procurement, purchasing, technical and fleet-side roles rather than assuming one title fits every purchase.

02

Need and timing signals

Use fleet additions, contracts, projects, retrofit, drydock and hiring signals as evidence for prioritization.

03

Actionable sales context

End the research with a priority, Why Now explanation and recommended next action.

Trust and limitations

Procurement intelligence should make uncertainty visible, not hide it.

Merqiva labels verified facts, inferences and unknowns. Decision-maker information can change, signals can be ambiguous and no research system can guarantee a reply or contract. The purpose is to improve sales prioritization and reduce wasted research time.
