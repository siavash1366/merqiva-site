Quality Promise

We guarantee the quality of the opportunity definition — not a customer's sales outcome.

Every delivered opportunity is designed to meet published qualification criteria. If an opportunity fails those criteria, the delivery can be reviewed for replacement under the service policy.

01

Qualification threshold

Opportunities are prioritized using company fit, product fit, fleet fit, buying-signal strength, recency, commercial relevance and timing.

02

Evidence-first

Key claims are separated into verified facts, inferences and unknowns. Sources are recorded so the customer can inspect the reasoning.

03

Replacement policy

If a delivered opportunity does not meet the agreed qualification criteria, Merqiva can replace the affected opportunity instead of pretending that every lead is equally valuable.

Important: This promise covers Merqiva's defined research and qualification criteria. It does not guarantee replies, meetings, contracts, revenue or a customer's sales performance.
