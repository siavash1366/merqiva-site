The difference

A buyer directory is not the same as a qualified sales opportunity.

A company can appear relevant because it operates vessels or works in the maritime sector and still be a poor fit for your equipment. Merqiva adds the layers a sales team needs before outreach: what the account operates, whether the product fits, whether something changed recently, who matters and how strong the evidence is.

Qualification framework

The sales question is not “Can this company buy?” It is “Is this account worth pursuing now?”

Merqiva evaluates company fit, product fit, fleet fit, signal strength, recency, decision-maker relevance and evidence quality. The output is designed to support prioritization — not to guarantee a purchase.
